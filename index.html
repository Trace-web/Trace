What makes Trace different

Your screen.
Your canvas.

A focused set of tools for adding visual explanations to the things already on your screen.

Draw over any app

Add visual annotations directly over the content on your screen. Highlight details, sketch ideas, and make explanations easier to follow.

Strokes that fade naturally

Keep your screen clear while you work. Configure how long drawing marks remain visible before they fade away.

Adjust drawing colour, stroke size, glow, fade duration, and keyboard shortcuts to suit the way you prefer to work.
